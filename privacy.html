Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Falu Tales Upload ("the tool") is used only by the creator of the Falu Tales channel to post their own videos to their own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

What data the tool handles

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens that YouTube and TikTok issue when the account owner authorises the tool.
  • Basic profile information of the authorised account (channel or account ID and display name), used only to confirm that uploads go to the correct account.
  • The video files, titles, descriptions and cover settings that the owner chooses to upload.

How it is stored and used

Tokens and profile information are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable only by the owner. Videos and their metadata are sent only to YouTube or TikTok, through their official APIs, when the owner starts an upload.

Sharing

The tool does not collect data from other people, has no server, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account permissions page and in the TikTok app settings (Security and permissions, Apps). Deleting the local token files also removes the tool's access.

Contact

Through the Falu Tales YouTube channel.
